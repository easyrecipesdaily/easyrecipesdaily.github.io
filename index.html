    Frequently asked questions

    It depends on the ingredient: a cup of flour weighs about 120 g, a cup of sugar about 200 g, and a cup of honey about 340 g. Use the converter above or the reference table — and for baking, weighing is always more accurate than using cups.
    Most savoury dishes double cleanly, but baking is fussier: leavening, salt and spices should not always be doubled exactly, and cooking time rises less than you expect. See our guide on scaling recipes for the details.
    350 °F is about 180 °C (gas mark 4). The converter above handles any oven temperature, and the reference table lists the common settings.
    Most cooked meals keep safely for 3–4 days in the fridge at or below 4 °C (40 °F). Cool food quickly, store it in shallow containers, and reheat it until steaming hot. When in doubt, throw it out.
    For one egg, try 3 tablespoons of unsweetened applesauce, half a mashed ripe banana, or 1 tablespoon of ground flaxseed mixed with 3 tablespoons of water and left for 5 minutes. The substitution finder above lists more swaps.
